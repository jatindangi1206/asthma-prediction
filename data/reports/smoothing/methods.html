How the settings were chosen

Every method assumes the same thing: an HRV reading is a slowly wandering level plus white noise. The level is what we want; the noise is what a smoother removes. Three numbers describe that, measured per patient from a robust variogram (how different two readings are as a function of the time between them) and then taken as the median across the cohort's patients:

Every derived setting is a translation of these numbers (plus cadence, circadian shape and volatility) into that method's own parameterisation. Nothing was searched or tuned. A setting that cannot be measured from the data says so and states why it keeps its value.
